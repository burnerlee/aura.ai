# Aura — brag plan (v2: pain → promise → product)

**What:** Aura is a calmer social feed that learns you through a friendly chat, ranks every post against your "Interest DNA", and keeps learning from each Resonate / Banish.
**For:** People burned out on doomscrolling feeds full of irrelevant, rage-baity or upsetting posts.
**Different:** Onboarding is a conversation, not a checkbox wall. Every feedback tap visibly moves your profile.
**Hook:** A grey, generic feed racing past (sponsored junk, outrage bait, "sensitive content" warnings) under the line "Tired of doomscrolling?"
**Angle:** Noise → quiet. The world goes from desaturated and frantic to Aura's calm dark UI with cyan accents. Music does the same: tense D minor → lifts into D major exactly at "Meet aura."
**Tone:** default, leaning polished. Soft dips, no hard cuts after the opener.
**Identity:** bg #020203/#0a0c0e, cyan #22d3ee, AI violet #a855f7; Manrope display, DM Mono labels. The real app DOM + CSS, captured from the app in demo mode.
**Data:** the live :5173 feed is empty, so the app was captured in demo mode (`VITE_USE_DEMO=1`). All authors and posts are the app's built-in fictional demo data. The "doomscroll" cards in scene 1 are illustrative generic posts made for this video, not from any real platform.
