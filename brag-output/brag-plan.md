# Aura — brag plan

**What:** Aura is a calm, personal feed: you chat with an AI for 20 seconds, it builds your "Interest DNA", and every post is ranked against it.
**For:** People tired of feeds that guess. **Different:** onboarding is a conversation, not a checkbox wall; each Resonate/Banish visibly moves your profile; under the hood posts are multi-vector sets ranked set-to-set (DESSERT-style), with LLM personas and no reinforcement learning.
**Hook:** "Feeds guess. Aura asks." **Visual hook:** the real chat modal answering, then the feed re-ranking (6% → 71% match chips), then a +7 AI/ML packet flying into Interest DNA.
**Tone:** default, leaning toward the app's own quiet, polished identity.
**Identity:** bg #0a0c0e, cyan #22d3ee, mint #9be9c5, AI violet #a855f7; Manrope (display), DM Sans (body), DM Mono (labels). Real app DOM + CSS, captured from the running app.
**Data:** live :5173 feed was empty, so captured from demo mode (:5174, `VITE_USE_DEMO=1`) — all authors/posts are the app's built-in fictional demo data. No user ids shown (demo avatar "AM").
